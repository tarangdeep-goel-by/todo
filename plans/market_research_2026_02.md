## Competitor Summary

### Todoist
- **Ramble** (Jan 2026): Voice-to-tasks via Gemini 2.5 Flash Live. 76K beta users, 290K sessions in 3 weeks. Task creation success ~62%. 38 languages.
- **Assist**: AI subtask generation, task rewriting
- Natural language parsing (best in class): "call John next Monday at 2pm p1"
- 5 levels of subtask nesting (Pro), karma gamification, 60+ integrations
- Pricing: Free / Pro $5/mo / Business $6/user/mo

### TickTick
- Built-in Pomodoro timer (17 white noise varieties)
- Built-in habit tracker (60+ habits)
- Eisenhower Matrix view
- Calendar views: Monthly, Weekly, Agenda, Yearly heatmap
- Best value: $3/mo annual

### Things 3
- Best-in-class UI/UX design (widely considered most beautiful)
- One-time purchase ($80 total across Apple devices)
- GTD methodology alignment
- Apple-only, no collaboration, no AI

### Microsoft To Do
- Completely free
- My Day: daily blank-slate with smart suggestions
- Deep M365 integration (Outlook flagged emails → tasks)
- Copilot integration via Planner

### Google Tasks
- Simplest option, zero learning curve
- Time-blocking in Google Calendar with busy status
- Completely free

### Any.do
- WhatsApp and email → task integration
- AI auto-prioritization (deadlines, effort, impact)
- Flat $5/user/mo

### Notion
- Multi-model AI (GPT-5, Claude Opus 4.1, o3)
- AI Agents (Notion 3.0): autonomous execution
- Infinitely flexible databases
- Plus $10/user/mo

### Superlist
- "Lists all the way down" — unlimited nesting
- AI Meeting Notes: transcribes Zoom/Meet → tasks (no visible bot)
- Email-to-task via Gmail label
- From Wunderlist creators

### Apple Reminders
- Apple Intelligence auto-categorizes reminders (iOS 26)
- Location-based reminders
- Siri context awareness (what's on screen)
- Completely free

## AI & Voice-First Trends

### Auto-Scheduling (Most Proven AI Feature)
- **Motion**: $550M valuation. Rebuilds schedule dozens of times daily across 1000+ parameters.
- **Reclaim.ai**: Auto-schedules tasks + habits + focus time. Claims 7.6hrs/week saved.
- **Trevor AI**: Personal AI model per user. Learns scheduling patterns over time.

### Voice-First (Exploding Category)
- **Todoist Ramble**: 76K users, 5x upgrade rate after first use. Validates demand.
- **tickk**: Open-source voice-first brain dump (ADHD-focused)
- **Vachi**: "Think-to-done" — talk to record, tap to organize, swipe when done
- **Sprout**: ADHD-focused brain dump → AI organizes, suggests priorities by energy level

### LLM-Powered Task Features
- Task decomposition: Todoist Assist, ClickUp Brain
- Context extraction: from docs, Slack, meetings, emails (Saner.AI, ClickUp Brain)
- Multi-model: ClickUp lets users toggle GPT-5/Claude/o3 per task

### Habit Tracking + Todo Convergence
- Reclaim.ai: habits as flexible calendar events
- TickTick: built-in habit tracker
- Tiimo: iPhone App of the Year 2025 (routines, ADHD)

### What Users Actually Want (vs Gimmicky)
**Valued**: Auto-scheduling, voice capture that works, task decomposition, calendar integration
**Gimmicky**: AI chatbots in task apps, over-automated systems, AI-generated descriptions

## Feature Gap Analysis (Ranked)

### Tier 1 — Proven High-Value
1. Calendar view + time-blocking (#1 requested across communities)
2. Smart reminders with due date intelligence
3. Task decomposition (voice: "break this down")

### Tier 2 — Strong Trend
4. Habit tracking
5. Recurring task templates (not just single tasks)
6. Start dates ("don't show until Monday")

### Tier 3 — Differentiating but Complex
7. AI auto-scheduling (requires calendar first)
8. Task extraction from email/Slack/meetings
9. Location-based reminders
10. Task dependencies (B blocked by A)

## Key Market Signals
- Voice-first is the next frontier (Todoist Ramble proves demand)
- AI Agents > AI Assistants (Notion 3.0, Motion "AI Employees")
- Calendar + Tasks are merging (Motion, Reclaim, Google)
- Hierarchical organization is a differentiator (most apps cap at 2 levels)
- Privacy-first AI matters (Todoist markets "audio not stored, SOC2")
